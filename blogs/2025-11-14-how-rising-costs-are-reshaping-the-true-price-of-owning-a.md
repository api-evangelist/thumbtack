If you own a home — or are dreaming of buying one soon — you probably know about the big expenses: your mortgage, utilities, and maybe even the occasional repair. But a new Zillow and Thumbtack analysis reveals that the hidden costs of homeownership are climbing faster than most people’s paychecks can keep up with.
