For years, the kitchen has been called the heart of the home. But today, it’s more than just a hub for cooking. It has become the backdrop for hosting, tackling daily to-dos, and even creating content. To better understand how kitchens are evolving, Thumbtack surveyed more than 1,000 homeowners across the country about how they actually use their kitchens and what they wish were different.
